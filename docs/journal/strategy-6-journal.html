Diagnosis
  • The green day is one signal. Strip GMRAIRPORT (+140k) and the other 7 signals are −76k. Do not read +64k as validation; do not read 5 SLs as failure. n=8, one dominant winner — almost no signal.
  • Low hit-rate is expected (offline study: target-first ~36%, edge carried by R:R, not win-rate). Today is a small, noisy instance of the predicted shape.
  • Stops are being hit by noise, not near-misses to target. Corrected check on the entry→exit window: stopped signals reached only 0–56% toward target before the SL (median 13%). SL distances are 0.22–0.62% — several (SUPREMEIND 0.22%, LTF 0.24%, ABCAPITAL 0.26%) sit inside normal intraday noise. The tight retest-swing stop, squeezed further by the lagged/higher fill, gets tagged before the trade can work. (Earlier whole-window "54–80%" numbers were wrong — they included post-SL dead-cat bounces.)
  • Partial-booking-at-50% is rejected as a fix. It caps winners (1.8R → 1.35R on a full win) and wouldn't have helped — only 1 of 7 losers reached 50% before stopping. Net-negative on this pattern.
Hypotheses to test (not yet acted on)
  • H1 — overlay lag tightens the geometry. Filling 25s late/higher makes the pinned structural SL relatively tighter + target farther. → the AI-overlay-off fix (built; deploy post-close) should reduce noise stop-outs.
  • H2 — the retest-swing SL is too tight for the noise band. → sweep sl_swing_buffer_pct / sl_atr_mult / a min-risk floor (~0.4–0.5%) on the S6 replay; measure expectancy vs stop-out rate. Cost: fewer lots per trade.
Actions taken today
  • Built the two-level AI-overlay kill switch (master + per-strategy). Deploy held — market was open; goes out post-close, then set breakout_retest.ai_overlay_enabled=false.
  • No live parameter changes (deliberate — one session is not a basis for tuning).
